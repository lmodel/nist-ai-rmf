# References

* [NIST AI 100-1](nist-ai-100-1.md) - NIST AI 100-1, the Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) of January 2023. lmodel/nist-ai-100-1 models it, and this schema holds that model.
* [NIST AI 600-1](nist-ai-600-1.md) - NIST AI 600-1, the Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile of the AI Risk Management Framework, of July 2024. lmodel/nist-ai-600-1 models it, and this schema holds that model.
* [lmodel/nist-ai-100-1](lmodel-nist-ai-100-1.md) - The lmodel repository that models the AI RMF as modules and records. This schema vendors all its modules and its catalogue at v0.1.0, and the weekly watch reports a later release.
* [lmodel/nist-ai-600-1](lmodel-nist-ai-600-1.md) - The lmodel repository that models the Generative AI Profile as modules and records. This schema vendors all its modules and its catalogue at v0.1.0, and the weekly watch reports a later release.
* [AI RMF to ISO/IEC 42001 crosswalk](iso42001-crosswalk.md) - The PDF that pairs each AI RMF subcategory with clauses and Annex B items of ISO/IEC FDIS 42001. Microsoft provided it, NIST's AI Resource Center lists it, and two scripts pin it by hash. It states no licence.
